🎥 Zakka Meet Pro - Complete User Guide

Welcome to Zakka Meet Pro

Zakka Meet Pro is a comprehensive video conferencing platform designed for personal, professional, and group communications. This guide will help you get started and explore all available features.

Getting Started

1. Creating Your Account

  • Sign Up: Click "Sign Up" to create a new account
  • Provide Information: Enter your email, username, and password
  • Gender Preference: Optionally select your gender preference for personalized experience
  • Language Selection: Choose your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese)
  • Confirmation: Verify your email to activate your account

2. Logging In

  • Enter your email and password
  • Your unique User ID will be displayed on your dashboard
  • You can now start or join meetings

Core Features

Video & Audio Controls

  • 🎙️ Mute/Unmute: Toggle your microphone on/off
  • 🎥 Camera: Enable/disable your camera
  • 🔄 Flip Camera: Switch between front and rear cameras
  • 🎛️ Audio Controls: Adjust microphone sensitivity and speaker volume

Meeting Management

  • Start Meeting: Create a new meeting room
  • Join Meeting: Enter a meeting with room ID or invite link
  • Group Conferencing: Support for up to 100+ participants (depending on plan)
  • Private Rooms: Create end-to-end encrypted private meetings
  • Meeting Settings: Configure max participants, duration, and privacy options

Communication Tools

  • 💬 Chat: Send messages during meetings
  • 🖥️ Screen Sharing: Share your screen with participants
  • 🎨 Video Filters: Apply filters to your video feed (grayscale, sepia, vivid, etc.)
  • 🤖 AI Subtitles: Real-time subtitle generation and translation

Recording & History

  • ⏺️ Record Meetings: Record video and/or audio
  • 📹 Recording Modes: Video+Audio or Audio-only recording
  • 📊 Call History: View all your past calls and meetings
  • 📁 Recording Library: Access and manage your saved recordings
  • ☁️ Cloud Storage: Automatic backup of recordings

Accessibility Features

  • 🔊 Screen Reader Support: Full compatibility with screen readers
  • 🎯 High Contrast Mode: Improved visibility for users with visual impairments
  • ⌨️ Keyboard Navigation: Navigate the entire application using keyboard
  • 🔤 Adjustable Font Sizes: Small, Normal, Large, Extra Large options
  • 🎯 Cognitive Simplicity Mode: Simplified interface for easier navigation
  • 🎨 Color Blind Modes: Support for various color blindness types

Welcome & Presence Features

Zakka Meet Pro provides welcoming messages and presence indicators to improve meeting awareness:

  • Welcome Message: New participants receive a private welcome message with meeting details and suggested settings.
  • System Announcements: Joins and leaves are broadcast as system messages in the chat for transparency.
  • Participant List: Active participants are visible via the participants count and optional participant panel.
  • Graceful Disconnect: When a participant disconnects unexpectedly, a system notice indicates the disconnect and auto-updates the participant list.

Troubleshooting

Common Issues

  • Camera/Microphone not working: Check browser permissions and device settings
  • Poor connection: Reduce video quality or close other applications
  • Audio feedback: Ensure microphone and speaker aren't in the same room
  • Can't join meeting: Verify meeting ID and check internet connection

Tips & Best Practices

  • ✅ Test your audio/video before important meetings
  • ✅ Use a stable internet connection (minimum 2.5 Mbps upload/download)
  • ✅ Enable screen reader before relying on accessibility features
  • ✅ Record important meetings for future reference
  • ✅ Use private rooms for sensitive discussions
  • ✅ Keep your software and browser updated
  • ✅ Use keyboard shortcuts for faster navigation
